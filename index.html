Eleanor Ashworth

Eleanor Ashworth is the author of seven novels and two collections of essays. Her work, often set along the coastal villages of the Pacific Northwest, explores inheritance, language, and the small inflections of family life. She is a former Whiting Award winner and her novel The Salt Glass was a finalist for the National Book Critics Circle Award. She lives in Port Townsend, Washington with her husband and two large, indifferent dogs.
