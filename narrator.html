Cassandra Lin

Audie-nominated voice for literary fiction.

Cassandra Lin is an Audie-nominated narrator with over 80 audiobook credits across literary fiction, memoir, and historical drama. Trained at the Juilliard School, she is known for her precise interior phrasing and a calm, lived-in voice that critics have called "ideal for the long novel." She has collaborated repeatedly with Eleanor Ashworth and Marlowe Bertram, and her recordings are produced from a home studio in Brooklyn.
